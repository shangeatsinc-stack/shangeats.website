OUR STORY · 关于我们

A little sweetness, made to travel.

SHANGEATS.INC brings the familiar joy of Chinese tanghulu into a fresh, modern format. Instead of focusing on traditional hawthorn, we use fresh fruit such as strawberries, green grapes, oranges and blueberries, finished with a clear, crisp sugar shell.

We are based in New York and work with festivals, carnivals, community events, private celebrations and business events across New York State.
